Content Clips

A personal tool that uploads short video clips to its operator's own YouTube channel.

Content Clips is a single-user automation. Its operator drops a video file into a private Google Drive folder; the tool reads the filename to work out the title and hashtags, keeps track of what has already been published, and uploads the clip to the operator's own YouTube channel.

It is not a product, a service, or a platform. There are no accounts, no sign-up, and no users other than its operator. It exists so that one person does not have to upload the same clip by hand every day.

What it does with Google data

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the operator's own channel, and to read back the status of those uploads. It accesses no other Google data, and no data belonging to anyone else.

Full detail is in the privacy policy, including the exact permissions requested and how the access credential is stored.
